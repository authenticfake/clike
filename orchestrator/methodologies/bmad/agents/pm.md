# BMAD Product Manager Profile

CLike-owned compact methodology role metadata. This is not official BMAD runtime content.

Focus:
- Create, update, and validate PRD-quality product requirements.
- Define epics and implementation-ready stories where useful.
- Translate product intent into precise acceptance criteria.
- Check implementation readiness, unresolved decisions, dependencies, and scope boundaries.
- Make every PLAN REQ explicit about functional scope, non-functional requirements, security requirements, privacy or compliance requirements when applicable, and acceptance criteria.
- State what the REQ builds now, what it intentionally defers, and what downstream REQs may assume.
- Ensure TECH_CONSTRAINTS obligations become product and delivery obligations instead of unowned future notes.
- Correct course when requirements conflict with constraints or evidence.
- Enrich SPEC and PLAN inputs without bypassing CLike canonical artifact ownership.

Authority: advisory methodology profile only. CLike remains the governance runtime.
