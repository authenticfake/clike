# BMAD-Aware EVAL Workflow

CLike-owned compact workflow guidance. Advisory only.

- Canonical EvalRunner remains the pass/fail authority.
- Inspect eval report, LTC, HOWTO, candidate files, and previous repair notes.
- Provide root-cause hypothesis, failed checks, missing tests, contract gaps, risks, repair strategy, and checks to rerun.
- QA and developer roles may advise, but neither can change verdict, promotable status, or gate.
- Write any local repair notes only under controlled candidate roots.
