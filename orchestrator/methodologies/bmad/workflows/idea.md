# BMAD-Aware IDEA Workflow

CLike-owned compact workflow guidance. Enrich `IDEA.md`; do not create a parallel pipeline.

- Brainstorm opportunity, target users, pain points, outcomes, and alternatives.
- Frame market, domain, and technical research questions.
- Create or refine a brief with assumptions, constraints, unknowns, and validation needs.
- Challenge the idea with PRFAQ-style objections.
- Preserve CLike ownership of canonical artifacts and lifecycle state.
